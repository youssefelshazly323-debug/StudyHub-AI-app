Can you explain the main difference between 3NF and BCNF with a simple example?
⚡
BCNF is strictly stronger than 3NF.

In 3NF, transitive dependencies of non-key attributes are eliminated, but we allow candidate keys to overlap. BCNF forbids this by requiring that every determinant is a superkey.
